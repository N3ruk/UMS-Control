# Platform artwork

Linux DEB and ZIP use the icon and wallpapers supplied and selected by N3ruk. The PNGs are preserved byte-for-byte from the supplied artwork. This inventory records provenance, not a claim that third-party imagery was created by the frontend author or is relicensed by the frontend GPL.

Windows uses the original geometric artwork in `assets/windows/`, generated by `tools/render_assets.py`; its provenance and hashes are recorded there. Classic Windows and High contrast are palette-only themes on both platforms.
